## Team fee: $155 per student

Every student pays a team fee to take part. It covers:

- Team registration and administrative expenses
- Program materials and student resources
- Team operations for the Fall session
- General program support for participating students

Competition travel is **not** included. Those payments are handled separately, and we'll announce when competition payment opens.

## How to pay

Payments are made through PayPal, and you can pay with Venmo from the same link. Before you start, sign in to your PayPal or Venmo account, and enter the student's full name **exactly as it appears on the student application** so we can match the payment to the right record.
