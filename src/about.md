## Our mission

Team Resistance exists to inspire and empower the next generation of scientists and engineers. Robots are how we do it, but the goal is skills that last well beyond robotics.

## What we value

- **Collaboration.** Nothing gets built alone. Students work together on every part of the robot and the team.
- **Innovation.** We encourage creative, out-of-the-box thinking.
- **Community engagement.** We do outreach with local schools and organizations across North Florida.
- **Learning and growth.** We learn as much from what fails as from what works.
- **Application.** Students take what they learn into everyday life, not just into the next robot.

## Our history

The program has run every year since 1996, when our first robot competed at the FIRST Nationals. Since then Team 86 has competed at more than 50 events, won five of them, qualified for the FIRST Championship nine times, and earned more than 30 awards. Explore every season on the [robots page](/history/) or the full [awards list](/awards/).

## Where we work

We build in the downtown Jacksonville machine shop and classroom provided by [Florida State College at Jacksonville](https://www.fscj.edu/), our longtime partner.
