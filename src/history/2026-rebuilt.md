## Build season

The program's 30th season started at kickoff and moved quickly into strategy, CAD and prototyping. The centerpiece of OHMER is a turret: the first version came together early in build season and was refined into a smoother mechanism before the robot shipped to its first event.

{% gallery photos.build, "Build season photos" %}

## Orlando Regional

In Week 3, OHMER ranked 15th after qualifications with a 5-5-0 record. The team joined Alliance 7 with 1902 Exploding Bacon and 4024 TechnoSaints and was eliminated in the second round of playoffs.

{% gallery photos.orlando, "Orlando Regional photos" %}

## Between events

Back in the shop, the team improved the feeding system, upgraded the shooter wheels and repaired wear across the robot.

{% gallery photos.between, "Between-events photo" %}

## Tallahassee Regional

The upgrades paid off in Week 7. OHMER finished qualifications ranked 6th at 8-4-0, then played on Alliance 4 with 8841 OTTER and 6184 C4, reaching the third round of playoffs.

{% gallery photos.tallahassee, "Tallahassee Regional photos" %}

## Offseason: Space Coast Showdown

At the one-day Space Coast Showdown in Titusville in September, OHMER ranked 4th of 30 teams with a 9-3-0 record. Alliance 3, with 8841 OTTER and a second robot from Pink, made it to round 5 and finished third, one step short of the finals. Four new driver pairs got their first real match experience at the event.
