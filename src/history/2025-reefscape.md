## Build season

The 29th year of the program, and the 27th as an FRC team, started with intake and control-board prototypes, followed by long stretches of testing and drive practice.

## Tallahassee Regional

A strong first event: OHMER ranked 7th, played on Alliance 4 with 4065 Nerds of Prey and 5872 Wiredcats, finished 4th in playoffs, and won the **Autonomous Award**.

## Orlando Regional

The team reached 3rd place in playoffs on Alliance 5 with 801 Horsepower and 6722 LED Robotics, and took home a second **Autonomous Award**.

## FIRST Championship

Regional performance earned a wildcard invitation to Houston. Among more than 600 teams, Team 86 competed in the 75-team Hopper Division and finished 49th against formidable opponents.

## Offseason: Space Coast Showdown

The team's best offseason result: a 6-1-0 record, 4th seed of 31 robots, and captaining Alliance 3 to a 4th-place finish in a six-alliance bracket.
