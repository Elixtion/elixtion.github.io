## Season overview

Team Resistance competed at the Tallahassee and Orlando Regionals in 2024 and attended the Space Coast Showdown in the offseason. The build went from kickoff and intake prototyping through shooter and climber testing to autonomous development.

## Results

OHMER-24 ranked 19th in Tallahassee and reached the third round of playoffs with Alliance 5. In Orlando the robot ranked 9th, and Alliance 4 finished 3rd, the team's best result of the season.
