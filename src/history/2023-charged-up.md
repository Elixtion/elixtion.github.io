## Season overview

2023 was Team Resistance's 25th year in FRC and 27th as a STEM and robotics program. The team competed at the Orlando and Tallahassee Regionals.

## Results

In Orlando, the robot ranked 8th at 10-5-0 and the team captained Alliance 6, picking 4118 and 1592, before being eliminated in round 5. In Tallahassee, it ranked 5th at 11-5-0 and played the playoffs with 386 and 1056, also reaching round 5.

The team won the **Industrial Design Award** in Orlando, and student Lana Gerstenberger was named a **FIRST Dean's List Finalist** in Tallahassee.
