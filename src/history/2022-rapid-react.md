## Season overview

Team Resistance won the **Tallahassee Regional** in 2022, along with the **Excellence in Engineering Award** there and the **Innovation in Control Award** at the Orlando Regional. The win qualified the team for the FIRST Championship in Houston, where it played in the Galileo Division.
